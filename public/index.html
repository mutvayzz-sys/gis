Did better than expected: 30.2 should be 151. Its percentages treated a small grade the same as a big one. Fixed: 69.1% made expected progress (was 69.1%). Result stays Weak.
  3. Phase 3 overall was added up wrong. It said 75.33 students made expected progress. That is the average of the grades, not the total. Added up, it is 226 out of 332. Did better than expected: 34.33 should be 103. Its percentages treated a small grade the same as a big one. Fixed: 68.1% made expected progress (was 67.6%). Result stays Weak.

Did better than expected: 48.25 should be 193. Its percentages treated a small grade the same as a big one. Fixed: 78.0% made expected progress (was 77.7%). Result stays Acceptable.

Build Simulations

What-if versions only. These are not actual results and should not be reported as findings.

The idea. A grade stops being Weak when at least 75 out of every 100 students make expected progress. Each option below pretends a few more students did, in as few grades as possible, until there are few enough Weak results.

Example. Grade 1 has 75 students and 50 made expected progress. To reach 75% it needs 57, so it is 7 short. If 7 more students had made it, Grade 1 would be Acceptable instead of Weak.

Phase results. A phase is all its grades added up, like a team score. Nobody changes it directly. It goes up by itself when its grades go up.

The fewest changes possible is 7 judgements. There are 83 ways to do that. The 6 that need the fewest students are shown, starting with the fewest students.

77 more ways use the same number of judgement changes but need more students. They are not shown.
