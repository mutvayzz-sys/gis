Build Simulations

What-if versions only. These are not actual results and should not be reported as findings.

The idea. A grade stops being Weak when at least 75 out of every 100 students make expected progress. Each option pretends a few more students did, in as few grades as possible, until there are few enough Weak results.

Example. A grade has 80 students and 52 made expected progress (65%). To reach 75% it needs 60, so it is 8 short. If 8 more students had made it, the grade would be Acceptable instead of Weak.

Phase results. A phase is all its grades added up, like a team score. Nobody changes it directly. It goes up by itself when its grades go up.
